The retained model, training, and evaluation implementation derives from RAEv2.
Its CC BY-NC 4.0 license and attribution are preserved in LICENSE.

The decoder and discriminator implementation includes attributed adaptations
from ViT-MAE (Transformers), taming-transformers, and StyleGAN-T. Source comments
retain those attributions. DINOv3 and EUPE are downloaded from their upstream
repositories at pinned revisions and use their respective model licenses.
SigLIP model weights are resolved by Transformers and use the upstream model license.
The optional GMuon optimizer is obtained from its upstream repository.

This distribution does not include third-party encoder weights, ImageNet images,
private cluster launchers, or upstream Git history.
